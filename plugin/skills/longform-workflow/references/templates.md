# Long-form templates

One section per content type. Stage 2 writes the outline to these sections (each heading with its intent and target words), and Stage 3 writes the draft to the same structure. A blog_post or guide is ContentForge's `standard` format; a listicle is a blog_post whose body H2s are the list items. The client's `Style rules` win over anything here: if their house style has its own blocks (a "Quick answer" box, an FAQ block, a demo banner), use theirs in place of the matching item below.

Every type starts the outline and draft Google Docs with these lines, which Stage 5 (Publish) carries into the Approved file:

- **Title:** under 60 characters where possible, with the keyword near the front.
- **Slug:** lowercase words from the keyword, joined by hyphens.
- **Meta description:** 140 to 155 characters, saying what the reader gets.
- **Author:** the byline as settled in Stage 1.

## blog_post (MVP)

1,200 to 1,800 words. One question, answered well.

1. **Opening answer:** 40 to 60 words that answer the title's question directly.
2. **Why it matters:** a short section tying the question to the reader's job, from `Audience and ICP`.
3. **Body:** 3 to 5 H2 sections, phrased as the questions the reader would ask (use the brief's People Also Ask lines). Each opens with its answer, then the detail, an example and any evidence.
4. **What to do next:** concrete steps or a checklist the reader can use.
5. **FAQ:** 3 to 5 questions from the brief, each answered in 2 to 4 sentences that stand on their own.
6. **Call to action:** one, matched to the funnel stage of the topic: subscribe or read more (awareness), a guide or webinar (consideration), a demo or contact (decision).

Required: the opening answer, at least one example from the client's world, every AI-visibility question in the brief answered, internal links.

## guide (MVP)

2,500 to 4,000 words. The reference page on a pillar's main term.

1. **Opening answer:** 50 to 80 words: what it is, who it's for, and what the guide covers.
2. **Contents:** a list of the H2 sections.
3. **Definition and context:** the term, why it matters now, and the common misconceptions.
4. **Body:** 5 to 8 H2 sections taking the reader from basics to practice. Use numbered steps for processes, a table for comparisons, and a checklist where the reader has to act.
5. **Mistakes to avoid:** the practitioner's view, from `SME profiles` where the SME has one.
6. **FAQ:** 5 to 8 questions.
7. **Call to action:** as for blog_post, usually consideration or decision.

Required: everything blog_post requires, plus at least one table or checklist, and a contents list that matches the H2s.
